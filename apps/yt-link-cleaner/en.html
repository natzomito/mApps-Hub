YT Link Cleaner

Strips tracking parameters from YouTube links — above all ?si=, which YouTube adds to every link you tap “Share” on, so it can tell who passed the link on and where. The video works exactly the same without them.

🕘 History

Cleaning runs 100% locally. History is stored only in your browser (localStorage). Video titles are fetched via noembed.com (requires internet). Written from scratch, no libraries.
